About

I'm Victoria — a builder, product thinker, and someone who believes technology is most interesting when it ships. This blog is where I share what I'm learning as I build with AI, ship software, and navigate the craft of product and project management.

What I write about

  • AI & LLMs — practical experience building with large language models, AI agents, and human-AI collaboration
  • Building Products — full-stack development, architecture decisions, and the messy reality of shipping
  • Product Management — frameworks, prioritization, discovery, and what actually works
  • Project Management — delivery, Lean/Agile, tooling, and getting things done
  • Tools & Workflows — the IDE, the terminal, the stack — whatever makes the work better

Why this blog

I keep a detailed personal knowledge base and journal as I work. Some of those insights are worth sharing. This blog is the public layer — ideas that survived the filter and might be useful to someone else building products with AI.

Everything here comes from real work, not theory. If I write about it, I've built with it, struggled with it, or shipped it.

Open knowledge

I maintain a public Cursor knowledge base — session learnings, onboarding guides, product management frameworks, and a glossary for non-developers working with AI tools. It's open source (CC BY 4.0) and designed as a starting point you can fork and make your own.
